# STRC guide — source and fact-check notes

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Primary-source facts are separated from the supplied Telegram copy and video transcript.

## Verified for the page
- Strategy's official STRC information page showed a 12.00% variable annualized rate for October 2026, semi-monthly cash dividends, monthly rate adjustment, and states that price/effective yield can vary and cash dividends are not guaranteed. Strategy says its corporate preferred securities are not collateralized by BTC and have a preferred claim on residual company assets.
- Strategy's 2026-10-12 release reported, as of 2026-10-11, USD Reserve $4.88B and USD Cash $640.5M; it also reported remaining buyback authorization. Thus the supplied post's $6.02B USD-assets/$3.8-year coverage is explicitly dated to 2026-09-27 and is historical, not current as of this page's review date. The guide avoids presenting it as current.
- RavilCrypto’s supplied VIP posts quoted PT-STRCx around 20–21% annualized (one post states 21.28%) before the 2026-11-26 maturity. Pendle market quotes move: search snapshots returned different values at different times. The guide attributes ~21% to the supplied post and tells readers to use the live quote, not treat it as current or guaranteed.
- Pendle’s official Discrete Yield documentation says STRCx is a discrete-yield listing; YT and LP rewards use time-weighting and payout rules, while “nothing changes” for PT and its fixed yield remains fixed. The page explains the PT route in plain language. Pendle’s App Guide says the user chooses an input asset on the Swap tab and reviews effective fixed APY after price impact and swap fees; there is no need to buy STRCx separately before buying PT.
- xStocks' official site states that xStocks are tokenized representations backed 1:1 under product terms; xStocks are unavailable to U.S. persons and in various restricted jurisdictions. Product disclosures say xStocks do not confer equity/shareholder/voting rights. Availability varies by jurisdiction and partner.
